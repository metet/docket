# Recommend MIT license and contributor copyright line

### Licensing Recommendation

1. **Licence: MIT**
   - Applies across the entire repository (both specification documents and the reference toolchain in `tools/`).
   - Short, universally recognised, and eliminates friction for adoption across different AI vendors, agents, and toolchains.
   - Avoids the administrative overhead and NOTICE file requirement of Apache-2.0 while fulfilling the open, cross-vendor interoperation goals.

2. **Copyright Line:**
   - Suggested copyright text:
     `Copyright (c) 2026 Docket contributors`
     (or naming the principal copyright holder, e.g. `Copyright (c) 2026 Mete Tunok`, as decided by `human`).
